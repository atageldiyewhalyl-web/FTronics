## The most important thing first

**The Next.js site in this repo is not what is live.** `https://www.ftsicherheitstechnik.com`
is still served by Apache as a hand-built static site with `.html` URLs
(`/loesungen-gewerbe.html`, `/produkt-detail-fn-32.html`). The rebuild uses clean,
extensionless URLs (`/loesungen-gewerbe`, `/produkte/fn-32`).

Every one of the 38 URLs in the live sitemap will 404 the day the new site goes up
unless a 301 map ships with it. That is the single highest-impact item in this audit,
and it is entirely preventable.

Worse: **six live pages have no equivalent in the rebuild at all.**

| Live URL | Words | New site equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| `/alarmanlagen-mannheim.html` | 844 | **none** — money keyword, no landing page |
| `/videoueberwachung-mannheim.html` | 831 | **none** — money keyword, no landing page |
| `/sicherheitstechnik-heidelberg.html` | 826 | **none** — local page |
| `/sicherheitstechnik-ludwigshafen.html` | 828 | **none** — local page |
| `/referenzen.html` | 327 | **none** — E-E-A-T / proof page |
| `/ratgeber.html` | 450 | `/ratgeber` (rebuilt, now better) |

Two of the client brief's primary keywords — *Alarmanlagen Mannheim* and *Videoüberwachung
Mannheim* — currently have dedicated, indexed landing pages that the rebuild deletes.
Redirecting them into `/loesungen-gewerbe` is a demotion: that page carries 308 rendered
words against their 830-ish.

## Critical (fix before launch)

### C1 · No redirect map for the URL migration — RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026
38 indexed `.html` URLs → new clean URLs. Without `301`s in the Apache/host config (or
`redirects()` in `next.config.mjs`), all accumulated ranking signal is dropped.

Mapping is mechanical for most of it:

```
/loesungen-gewerbe.html          → /loesungen-gewerbe
/loesungen-privat.html           → /loesungen-privat
/produkte.html                   → /produkte
/produkt-detail-<slug>.html      → /produkte/<slug>
/produkt-detail-fb8a.html        → /produkte/fb-8a-pro      (note: irregular)
/produkt-detail-fc8d.html        → /produkte/fc-8d-pro      (note: irregular)
/faq.html, /kontakt.html, /support.html, /partner.html,
/ueber-uns.html, /karriere.html, /konfigurator.html,
/ratgeber.html, /impressum.html, /datenschutz.html, /agb.html → same without .html
```

The four remaining ones have no clean target and need a decision (see C2).

### C2 · Four ranking pages are being deleted
`/alarmanlagen-mannheim`, `/videoueberwachung-mannheim`, `/sicherheitstechnik-heidelberg`,
`/sicherheitstechnik-ludwigshafen`. Rebuild them as routes in the new site before launch
(they map cleanly onto the service + service-area structure), or accept the loss knowingly.
Redirecting a keyword-targeted page into a generic hub loses the keyword.

The `/referenzen` page should also be rebuilt — it is the only proof-of-work surface on
the site, and E-E-A-T for a B2B installer leans on exactly that.

## High priority

### H1 · 35 of 39 titles exceed 60 characters — RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026
The root layout appends `" | FT Sicherheitstechnik"` via `title.template`. Combined with
already-long page titles, almost everything truncates in SERPs. Product pages are worst:

```
87  FTronics FB-8A Pro: 4K Bullet-Kamera mit vollständiger KI-Suite | FT Sicherheitstechnik
86  FTronics FC-8D Pro — 4K Dome IP-Kamera ... | FT Sicherheitstechnik
85  FTronics FB-8A Max: 4K Bullet-Kamera mit aktiver Abschreckung | FT Sicherheitstechnik
83  Zutrittskontrolle: RFID, PIN, App, Biometrie | FT Sicherheitstechnik   (blog)
75  Sicherheitstechnik für Gewerbe & Industrie | FT Sicherheitstechnik Mannheim
```

Fix: shorten `metaTitle` in `src/lib/product-page-data*.js` to ~40 chars and let the
template add the brand, or use `title: { absolute: … }` on product routes and drop the
suffix. The blog packages shipped with ≤60-char `title_tag` values; the implementation
appends the brand on top of them in `src/app/ratgeber/[slug]/page.jsx:19` — that line
should use the `title_tag` as-is.

### H2 · No Open Graph or Twitter image anywhere except blog posts
`src/app/layout.jsx` sets `openGraph` without `images`, and no `twitter` block at all.
34 of 39 pages share links with no preview image. Add a default 1200×630 OG image in the
layout (`openGraph.images`) plus `twitter: { card: 'summary_large_image' }`, then override
per product page with the product hero.

### H3 · Self-serving `aggregateRating` on all 39 pages — RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026
`localBusinessJsonLd()` in `src/lib/site.js:141` emits `aggregateRating` 5.0 / 19 reviews,
and the layout injects it site-wide. Google's structured-data policy disallows review
markup a business writes about itself; it is ineligible for rich results and is a manual-action
risk. The rating is also attached to pages that display no reviews at all (every product page).

Fix: remove `aggregateRating` from the shared entity. If you want the rating visible, keep it
as on-page content on the homepage only (it already is, `src/app/page.jsx:684`) and let Google
pull the rating from the Google Business Profile.

### H4 · 115 MB of assets in `public/`, 86 MB of it PNG — RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026
- 80 files over 300 KB; largest single file 2.3 MB
- **38 of the 51 `magnific_*.png` upscaler outputs are unreferenced** — roughly 55 MB of dead
  weight sitting in the deploy
- `public/camera video.mp4` (2.0 MB, space in the filename) is unreferenced.
  *(Correction: an extensionless `public/camera` was also reported here — it never
  existed; the measurement split `camera video.mp4` on the space.)*

Delete the unreferenced files, then convert the remaining PNG photography to WebP/AVIF.

### H5 · No `next/image` anywhere except the navbar logo — PARTIALLY RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026 (LCP elements done, 25 secondary `<img>` remain)
39 raw `<img>` tags across the app, including every hero and product shot. No `width`/`height`
(layout shift → CLS), no responsive `srcset`, no automatic format negotiation. The blog post
hero at `src/app/ratgeber/[slug]/page.jsx` is a bare `<img>` in the largest above-the-fold slot
on the page — that is the LCP element on five pages.

### H6 · The blog is not linked from anywhere on the site
`grep` finds **zero** `href="/ratgeber/<slug>"` links outside the `/ratgeber` section itself.
The five new posts are reachable only through the listing page. Each content package shipped
with an inbound-link plan (`/loesungen-gewerbe` service cards, `/faq` sections, product-page
CTAs) — none of it was implemented. Those links are how the posts pass equity to the money
pages and vice versa.

## Medium priority

### M1 · Meta description lengths are off across the board
Homepage 202 chars (truncates), `/impressum` 68, `/agb` 120, and 13 of 18 product pages sit at
115–140 where 140–155 is the usable range. Product descriptions are auto-derived from
`product-page-data*.js` — fix once at the source.

### M2 · `robots.js` blocks pages that are already `noindex` — RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026
`src/app/robots.js` disallows `/impressum`, `/datenschutz`, `/agb`, and those routes *also*
set `robots: { index: false }`. The `Disallow` prevents Google from ever reading the `noindex`.
Pick one — drop the `Disallow` and keep the meta directive.

Separately: for a German site, `Impressum` is a trust signal. Consider indexing it.

### M3 · No `Service` schema on the solutions pages — RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026
The site has `LocalBusiness`, `BreadcrumbList` (every page), `Product` (all 18 product
pages, via `standard-product-page.jsx`), `FAQPage`, `JobPosting`, and `BlogPosting` — genuinely
good coverage. The gap is `Service` entities for the six commercial disciplines, which is what
would connect the money pages to the `hasOfferCatalog` already declared in the LocalBusiness entity.

### M4 · Award seals carry empty alt text — CORRECTION, not a bug (see below)
`src/app/page.jsx:422` and `:435` — `plus-x-award-2026.jpg` and `dipmb-kundenzufriedenheit-2024.jpg`
both have `alt=""`. The other 12 empty alts in the codebase are correctly-decorative and
commented as such; these two are trust signals and should be described.

### M5 · No `llms.txt` — RESOLVED 27 Aug 2026
Returns 404 live. For a business whose customers increasingly ask ChatGPT and Perplexity
"Sicherheitsfirma Mannheim", a `/llms.txt` listing the services, service area and key
pages is cheap. The blog content is already well-structured for citation (direct answers in
the first 150 words, FAQ blocks, `FAQPage` schema).

## What is already good

Worth stating plainly, because it is a lot:

- **Clean build.** 43 routes, all statically prerendered, ~103–115 KB First Load JS. No client
  components on route pages. This is a fast architecture, undermined only by the image weight.
- **Canonicals on every route**, correctly self-referencing, via `metadataBase` + `alternates`.
- **Exactly one H1 per page** across all 39 pages.
- **Schema depth is above average for the sector** — `LocalBusiness` with `GeoCircle`
  `areaServed` at 50 km, hours, geo, awards, founder; `BreadcrumbList` on every page;
  `Product` on all 18 product pages; `FAQPage`; `JobPosting` on `/karriere`.
- **Business facts are centralised** in `src/lib/site.js` — NAP consistency is structurally
  guaranteed rather than hoped for.
- **HTTPS with HSTS**, `http → https → www` chained correctly, `X-Frame-Options` set.
- **The five new Ratgeber posts** render at 1,190–1,556 words with 9–11 H2s, valid
  `BlogPosting` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` graphs, OG images and correct canonicals.
